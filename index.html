Why we built The Phenomenon Project

We bring together extraordinary human experiences to explore patterns, discover connections, and build a resource for anyone seeking a better understanding of the Phenomenon.

What we mean by “the Phenomenon”

We welcome UAP sightings, entity encounters, near-death experiences, psi experiences, altered states, and other unexplained events. Psi includes apparent telepathy, precognition, and remote perception; altered states can include meditation, dreams, and psychedelic experiences. Categories can overlap and do not imply a shared cause.

Sharing your experience

Tell us what happened in your own words. Include the context, note uncertainties, and distinguish observations from interpretations. Supporting material is welcome but never required. Submissions are for adults 18 and older.

Understanding the data

Approved reports contribute to the archive and its patterns. Review checks suitability for publication; it does not verify an experience or its explanation.

The data describes this collection of self-reported accounts. Patterns can suggest useful questions, but cannot establish causes or how common an experience is in the wider population. Counts represent reports, not necessarily different people or events.

Privacy Policy

What you share

The Phenomenon Project collects the account, structured answers, optional pseudonym and supporting-material links you submit, together with a report ID, submission time, review status and your consent choices. We accept submissions only from people aged 18 or older. Please avoid exact addresses, names, medical records and information identifying other people. A narrative, location or linked file may identify you even without your name.

Who can see your report

The designated owner can review the full submission. After approval, the structured answers are available to visitors who can access the site and can be included in charts and member CSV downloads. These include date, broad location, categories, features, context, witnesses and structured psi answers. Small groups or unusual combinations can still identify someone; omit details you are uncomfortable sharing.

Your title, narrative, interpretation, pseudonym, free-text psi follow-ups and supporting links appear in the archive only if you separately choose to share the full account. Earlier submissions used a consent statement permitting full publication. If the site becomes publicly accessible, approved material shared for publication may be viewed and copied by anyone. The structured data is accessible through the archive even though the charts and download controls require sign-in.

Hosting, sign-in and spam protection

The site uses OpenAI’s Sites hosting and Cloudflare infrastructure. Hosting and sign-in services process technical information under their own policies, including information needed to deliver and secure the service. ChatGPT sign-in identifies you to the hosting platform. The application does not attach your member identity to an encounter report; this is not a guarantee of complete anonymity.

The application uses a keyed, daily-changing representation of your network address for separate spam counters; it does not store raw network addresses in report records. Limits expire within a day, and expired counters are cleaned up on later submissions. If Cloudflare human verification is enabled, it processes a browser check. See OpenAI’s privacy policy and Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Purpose and retention

We use submissions to review accounts, operate the archive, and explore patterns. Reports remain in the application database until withdrawn or removed, including reports awaiting review or rejected from publication. Consent records are retained with the report. Legal obligations may require preservation or disclosure. We cannot promise secrecy against valid legal process.

Withdrawal and concerns

Save the receipt and private withdrawal code. “Withdraw a report” deletes the report from the live application and future analysis. It cannot recall other people’s downloads, screenshots, cached copies or material on external services. Infrastructure backup and log retention is controlled by the service providers; withdrawal does not promise immediate deletion from their backups.

Use the “Privacy / copyright concerns” form if you lose your code, find identifying information, believe a submission is from a minor, or need to raise a rights issue. Describe only what is necessary. Concerns are visible only to the owner and remain until the owner deletes them; they are not published or included in encounter data. The form supplies a reference number but does not send email or provide a reply inbox. Identity or ownership may need to be established before a request can be acted on. This service is not monitored continuously.

External material

Supporting material is linked, not uploaded here. Following a link takes you to another service, whose privacy practices apply. File metadata, account names, faces, voices and locations can identify people. Remove private details before sharing.

Submission Terms

Eligibility and agreement

You must be at least 18 to submit. By checking the required agreement, you accept these terms and acknowledge the Privacy Policy. Describe your experience honestly, distinguish observations from interpretations and note uncertainty. You do not need to establish a particular explanation.

Permission to use your submission

You retain ownership of your original material. You grant The Phenomenon Project a nonexclusive, royalty-free permission to store and review your submission and, after approval, display and analyze its structured answers and include them in structured-data exports. If you select the separate full-account sharing option, this permission also covers displaying your title, narrative, interpretation, pseudonym, free-text psi answers and supporting links. This permission is limited to operating the archive and its stated research purposes.

You may withdraw using your receipt. Withdrawal ends future publication and analysis of that report by the live site, subject to necessary legal retention; already downloaded or independently copied material cannot be recalled. Data access does not grant permission to harass participants, attempt to identify them, or infringe anyone’s rights.

Respect others’ rights

Share only material you created or have permission or another lawful basis to share. Do not submit private contact details, identifying accusations, threats, harassment, unlawful recordings, confidential documents, or material you are prohibited from disclosing. Supporting material is optional. Permission to share a link does not transfer ownership of the linked content.

Review and interpretation

The owner may decline or remove reports for privacy, rights, safety, spam or relevance concerns. Approval is a community review decision, not independent verification of an event or explanation. Categories describe reports and do not establish a common cause. Voluntary submissions are not a representative population sample; patterns cannot establish prevalence or causation.

Health and safety

The Phenomenon Project is an informational archive, not medical care, diagnosis, treatment or emergency assistance. Do not use its reports to stop prescribed treatment or undertake unsafe activities. Seek qualified help for health concerns. For an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services. Submissions and concerns are not monitored continuously.

Privacy and copyright concerns

Use the linked concern form to identify the report or material and explain the issue. For copyright concerns, identify the work and the allegedly infringing link and describe your relationship to the rights holder. This is an owner-review channel; we do not represent it as a registered DMCA agent service. Keep private withdrawal codes out of messages.

Changes

The effective date identifies this policy version. Your submission records the terms and sharing choices accepted at that time. A later policy revision does not by itself expand permission to publish a previously private account.

Privacy / copyright concerns

Send a concern privately to the site owner. Include the report ID or title and only the details needed to locate the issue. Do not include a withdrawal code, identity documents or sensitive medical information. This form does not send email or offer a reply inbox and is not monitored continuously.

By sending, you ask the owner to store and review this concern as described in the Privacy Policy.

Withdraw your report

Enter the details from your receipt. This permanently deletes the report from the archive and future analysis; existing downloaded copies cannot be recalled.

Owner workspace

Review submissions

Approve only after checking the account and every structured field. Approval puts structured answers into the archive and analysis; full text and links appear only with separate publication permission.

Review checklist

  1. Privacy: Check names, exact places, contact details, health details, and identifying information about others in the story, structured fields, and linked material. Reject or hold a report if it cannot be safely published as submitted.
  2. Quality and safety: Look for spam, harassment, impersonation, prohibited material, or too little detail to understand the account. Approval means it meets publication rules, not that it was verified.
  3. Possible duplicate event: Compare date, broad location, and description with existing reports. A similar event may have several witnesses. Leave uncertain cases pending; do not treat report counts as independent events.
  4. Sharing choice: Confirm whether full-account publication was selected. Without it, only approved structured fields should be visible.

Use the Google Sheet connected backend to approve, mark pending, or reject records.
