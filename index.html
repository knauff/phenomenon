About the Project

The Phenomenon Project collects, normalizes, and analyzes non-ordinary human experiences. By taking firsthand accounts seriously without dogmatic commitment, we identify cross-domain structural patterns.

Traditional science frequently dismisses anomalous occurrences due to lack of reproducible instrumentation. Our approach treats conscious observer reporting as signal rather than noise, compiling unified datasets across distinct experiential domains.

The complete, anonymized repository data ledger is compiled in real-time and made freely available under open public licenses for independent statistical analysis and structural charting.

Privacy Protocol

The Phenomenon Project is built on strict data minimization and total contributor anonymity.

Zero Identifiers: We do not capture IP addresses, device identifiers, email addresses, or geographic coordinate telemetry.

Data Redaction: Automated ingestion pipelines sanitize accounts to remove mentioned proper names, phone numbers, or residential addresses prior to ledger persistence.

Open Verification: Ingested entries are routed directly to public research tables under open community ownership.

Terms of Participation

By contributing an experiential report to this repository, you agree to the following scientific commons framework:

1. Public Domain Contribution: All narrative descriptions are published under the Creative Commons Public Domain Dedication (CC0 1.0 Universal).

2. Authenticity: You warrant that submissions represent genuine personal accounts to the best of your recollection, free of intentional fabrication.

3. Age Requirement: Contributors must be 18 years of age or older at the time of submission.

Privacy & Copyright Concerns

If an account contains identifying personal details or copyrighted text submitted without authorization, submit a removal request immediately.

Contact the repository maintainers directly with the report headline, submission timeframe, and removal justification. Requests are reviewed within 24 hours.

Withdraw a Report

Because accounts are submitted without email addresses, removal requests require providing the exact headline, submission date, and distinctive phrasing from the account body.

Maintainers compare verifiable textual fingerprints against the ledger before striking the row from all public distribution feeds.

Owner Review & Governance

Ledger moderation guidelines and governance rules applied by project stewards:

1. Trolling & Abuse: Obvious spam, malicious harassment, or automated commercial garbage is permanently purged at the ingestion gate.

2. Structural Neutrality: Accounts are never altered, editorialized, or suppressed based on personal philosophical, materialist, or metaphysical viewpoints.
