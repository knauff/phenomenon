Why we built Anomalous Accounts

We bring together extraordinary human experiences to explore patterns, discover connections, and build an open research resource for anyone seeking a better understanding of the anomalous.

What we mean by "Anomalous"

We welcome UAP sightings, entity encounters, near-death experiences, psi experiences, altered states, and other unexplained events. Psi includes apparent telepathy, precognition, and remote perception; altered states can include meditation, dreams, and unitive experiences. Categories can overlap and do not imply a shared cause.

Sharing your experience

Tell us what happened in your own words. Include the context, note uncertainties, and distinguish observations from interpretations. Supporting material is welcome but never required. Submissions are for adults 18 and older.

Understanding the data

Approved reports contribute to the open archive and its patterns. Review checks suitability for publication; it does not verify an experience or its explanation.

The data describes this collection of self-reported accounts. Patterns can suggest useful questions, but cannot establish causes or how common an experience is in the wider population. Counts represent reports, not necessarily different people or events.

Your privacy and choices

No name, email, or account is required to submit. Approved structured answers appear in the archive and data downloads. Your written narrative and supporting links are published only if you separately opt in to share them. Avoid identifying details about yourself or others.

Keep your private submission receipt code to withdraw your report at any time. Copies already downloaded by others cannot be recalled. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Privacy Policy

What you share

Anomalous Accounts collects the account, structured answers, and telemetry you submit. We accept submissions only from people aged 18 or older. Please avoid exact addresses, names, medical records, and information identifying other people. A narrative, location, or linked file may identify you even without your name.

Who can see your report

After review, structured telemetry answers (date, broad location, categories, signatures, setting, witness count) are made available publicly for research charting and open CSV downloads. Your narrative is published only if you explicitly choose the full-account sharing option.

Withdrawal and concerns

Keep your private receipt code. You can withdraw your report at any time using the withdrawal form, permanently purging it from the live ledger and future research distributions.

Effective September 22, 2026

Submission Terms

Eligibility and agreement

You must be at least 18 to submit. Describe your experience honestly, distinguish observations from interpretations, and note uncertainty. You do not need to establish a particular explanation.

Permission to use your submission

You retain ownership of your original observations. You grant Anomalous Accounts nonexclusive permission to store, review, and display approved structured telemetry for research and public analysis.

Privacy / copyright concerns

Send a concern privately to the site owners. Include the report ID or title and only the details needed to locate the issue. Do not include a withdrawal code or identity documents.

Withdraw your report

Enter the details from your receipt. This permanently marks the report as withdrawn and removes it from future research datasets.

Owner workspace

Review submissions

Approve only after checking the account and every structured field. Approval places structured answers into the archive and live research download.

Review checklist

  1. Privacy: Verify no personal names, addresses, or private contact info appear in text fields.
  2. Quality: Confirm report meets publication rules and distinguishes observation from speculation.
  3. Full Narrative Permission: Check whether contributor checked full narrative publication.
